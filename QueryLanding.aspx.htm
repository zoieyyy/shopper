If you cannot see any available jobs on the Job Board, it means there are no jobs within a 20KM radius from your home address.

You can update your location or expand the KM radius by clicking on the Filter Bar and viewing the respective filter options.

You can easily filter the Job Board by job type — for example, you may only want Restaurant visits, or Online visits.

Online jobs show at your home address, therefore they will always appear at the top of the Job Board.

If you do not want to see Online Visits, or would like to remove them so you can see other available jobs simply:

  1. Click on Filter on the Job Board
  2. Expand the Job Categories
  3. Click jobs of interest and exclude any Online categories
